I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow at the Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto, advised by Professor [Ming Hu](http://individual.utoronto.ca/minghu/). Before this, I completed my Ph.D. at the Department of Industrial Systems Engineering and Management, National University of Singapore, where I was advised by Associate Professor [Xiaobo Li](https://sites.google.com/site/lixiaobohome/home). 
<br><br>
Much of my research studies decision-making with limited information, spanning inventory management, pricing and revenue management, and the design of markets and mechanisms. I am also interested in broader operations management problems, including resource allocation, service systems, and agentic commerce.
